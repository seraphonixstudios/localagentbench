# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):

- Be respectful and constructive; critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask for clarification before concluding malice.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks — in issues, PRs, or results discussions.
- Benchmark debates stay technical: methodology over marketing.

Maintainers may remove contributions or contributors that violate these principles. Reports: open a private issue or contact the maintainers through the repository profile.
